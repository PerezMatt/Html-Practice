Ham and cheese Sandwich

Ingredients

Sliced Bread
Any bread would do, whole, white, wheat
Mayo
Store brought
Mustard (optional)
Tomatoes
Can be roma or globe/slicer
Onions
Can be red, white, yellow
lettuce
Can be romaine or iceburg
Ham
Can almost any kind, original, black forest, honey, spicy
Cheese
Any Cheese can do, american, cheddar, jack

Preparation

  1. Grab your favorite bread
  2. Put mayo on two pieces of bread
  3. Add ham and a slice of cheese
  4. Add slices of tomatoes, onions, and a piece of lettuce
  5. Slice in half or eat as is. enjoy
